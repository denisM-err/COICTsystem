About CoICT

Collage of information and Communication Technologies (CoICT) is a collage of the University of Dar es salaam dedicated to education, research and innovation in information and communication technologies

Our Vision

To be a leading of excellence in information and communication technologies education, research and innovation

Our Mission

To provide quality education, conduct innovative research and offer ICT-based solution that contribute to national and global development

What CoICT Offers

  • Undergraduate Education
  • Postgraduate Education
  • Research and innovation
  • Information and Communication Technologies Trainings

Location

CoICT is located at Kijitonyama, Dar es salaam Tanzania
